Simulated tabletop pick-and-place

PickPlace1B

A Franka pick-and-place dataset rendered in Isaac Sim, heading for one billion RGB frames. Every number on this page is counted from the saved episodes.

Progress

Saved successful episodes only. An episode is one pick followed by one place, recorded by five RGB + depth cameras.

target 1,000,000,000 RGB frames

Render speed

Episodes saved per clock hour since the dataset began. One worker is one GPU running eight parallel slots per round.

Measured rate per worker

Worker-hours are the summed wall time of all production rounds, including rounds that saved nothing (no successful first pass, or no feasible table). Frames per episode is the verified mean.

Per-slot backgrounds and table textures

A round renders eight slots in parallel. Since 14 September each slot gets its own floor and walls, and since 15 September its own table texture; earlier episodes share one background and texture per round.

Dome light intensity

The same scene rendered at eight intensities across the production range 0 to 8000 (quality rendering mode, fixed yaw, no HDRI).
